Why Choose Smart Carpool?

🧠 Smart Ride-Matching

Multi-criteria matching engine evaluates route compatibility (50%), departure time window (20%), cost efficiency (20%), and seat availability (10%) to rank the most compatible rides.

💰 Fair-Split Sharing

No surge pricing. Drivers set estimated fuel and toll expenses. Costs are divided transparently among occupants with configurable driver contribution options.

🗺️ Interactive OpenStreetMap

Explore origin, destination, and intermediate pickup stops rendered via Leaflet.js and OpenStreetMap. Clear visual routes with zero expensive proprietary API keys.

How It Works

For Drivers

  1. Register your vehicle (Make, Model, License Plate, Capacity).
  2. Publish your upcoming travel route, departure time, and stops.
  3. Set the trip expense; our calculator automatically splits the fare.
  4. Review passenger booking requests and confirm seats.
  5. Mark the trip completed and earn passenger ratings.

For Passengers

  1. Enter your origin and destination to search available rides.
  2. Review ranked matches with transparent compatibility scores.
  3. Confirm your seat reservation with atomic overbooking protection.
  4. Receive in-app status updates for your booking.
  5. Share the journey, reduce travel costs, and rate your driver.
